# Why Am I Seeing This?
If you are seeing this it is because you are using BingoHelper on a version of Minecraft that it no longer supports. To ensure BingoHelper works as intended, please follow these steps:.

1. Download the [latest version](https://github.com/LilFroggy/BingoHelper/releases/latest) of BingoHelper
2. Download [Prism Launcher](<https://prismlauncher.org/>) and sign in to your Minecraft account.
3. Click "Add Instance"
4. Set Version to the version of the jar you downloaded and Mod Loader to "Fabric" then click OK
5. Select the instance you just created
6. Click "Edit" then "Mods"
7. Drag in the BingoHelper jar you downloaded
7. Click "download mods" and download [Fabric API](<https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api>) and [Fabric Language Kotlin](<https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-language-kotlin>).
8. Launch!
